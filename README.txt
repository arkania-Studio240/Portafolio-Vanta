VANTA CREATIVE STUDIOS — Thumbnail portfolio
Files: index.html, styles.css, script.js, images/ (full size) and images/thumbs/ (720px cards).
Keep them together when publishing.
Every image in images/ has the VANTA watermark + logo baked in. Keep your clean originals OUT of the published site
(they are in originals-private/, do not upload that folder).

Add a project: put the 3 images in images/ and copies resized to 720x405 in images/thumbs/ (same file names),
then add an entry to PROJECTS in script.js. Add a category: add it to CATEGORIES and to each language in I18N.
